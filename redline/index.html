    On board Redline I

    Eight and a half months, done properly.

    The ship is built for one job: getting forty-eight people to Mars rested, fed and ready to walk.

    1. 48

      PassengersForty-eight seats a window and six crew. Everyone has a cabin to themselves.

    2. 0.38g

      GravityThe habitat ring spins to match Mars, so you step off already used to the weight.

    3. 2.4m

      Your cabinWall to wall, with a bunk, a desk and a real window.

    4. 6kg

      LuggageEverything else is waiting in the habitat. Pack what you would miss.
